TASK 2 — INTERVIEW QUESTIONS AND SHORT ANSWERS

1. What is phishing?
Phishing is a type of social engineering in which an attacker uses deceptive messages to trick people into revealing information or taking an unsafe action.

2. How can you identify a phishing email?
Look for an unexpected message, an unverified sender, urgent or threatening language, unusual links, unexpected attachments, generic greetings, and spelling or grammar mistakes.

3. What is email spoofing?
Email spoofing is the falsification of email sender information so that a message appears to come from a different address or organization.

4. Why are phishing emails dangerous?
They can trick recipients into disclosing sensitive information, visiting unsafe websites, or opening harmful attachments.

5. How can you verify sender authenticity?
Check the full sender address and relevant header information, but do not rely on one indicator alone. Verify the request using a known official website or contact method.

6. What tools can analyze email headers?
Email clients may provide a “show original” or “view message source” option. Header-analysis tools can help interpret authentication and routing fields. Use only approved tools and avoid uploading private data without permission.

7. What should you do with a suspected phishing email?
Do not click links or open attachments. Report it using the email provider's phishing-report option or to the organization's IT/security team, then follow their instructions.

8. How do attackers use social engineering in phishing?
They may imitate trusted organizations or use urgency, fear, curiosity, or authority to influence a recipient's decisions.
